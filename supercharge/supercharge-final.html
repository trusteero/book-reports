Supercharge by David Norman

Making Operating Systems Stick Through Community-Enhanced Leadership

Supercharge is a practical leadership and operations playbook for founders, operators, and consultants using EOS/Traction or Scaling Up who find that execution systems alone produce compliance rather than commitment. The book argues that durable performance comes from pairing rigorous operating rhythms, metrics, and accountability tools with deliberate community-building practices such as truth-telling, participation, and shared ownership. It provides audits, scripts, checklists, and side-by-side framework guidance designed to make operating systems culturally sustainable rather than mechanically installed.
