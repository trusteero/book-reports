The Broken Crown by Jesse Karjalainen

A relic unearthed. A truth concealed. A legacy reclaimed.

When a grieving widower's night-time search across the fields of Wiltshire uncovers a fragment of King Alfred's crown, he becomes entangled in a web of royal secrecy, modern violence, and buried truths that refuse to stay hidden. The Broken Crown weaves contemporary investigation with echoes of England's past, exploring power, loss, and the cost of revelation in a world still shaped by its history.

Key Characteristics
Educated, culturally engaged readers aged 25–65 with strong interest in history, mystery, and investigative storytelling. The Broken Crown appeals strongly to readers who value intelligent, emotionally resonant mysteries grounded in real-world settings. Its dual appeal spans a digitally active 25–54 audience—urban professionals, journalists, and academics—and an older 45–65 readership of heritage enthusiasts and royal-history devotees. Ebook and audiobook consumption dominate, with library memberships and podcast discovery providing sustained reach across global English-speaking markets.

Analysis Overview

Readership
Classification
Positioned as a royal-history mystery with investigative and archaeological threads, The Broken Crown draws from a bespoke subgenre taxonomy encompassing Treasure Hunt, Cold Case Mystery, and Alternate History motifs. It occupies a refined space between literary suspense and commercial thriller, offering a measured, atmospheric take on heritage intrigue.
Personas
Charlotte Hensley, an academic reader attuned to moral complexity and historical fidelity, embodies the book's reflective core, while Daniel Okoro, a journalist hungry for truth and ethical tension, mirrors its investigative heartbeat. Together, they illustrate a readership that craves both intellect and emotional resonance.

Marketing Strategy

Pre-Launch

6 weeks pre-launch (awareness)

  • Introduce the novel through striking imagery and narrative hooks evoking royal mystery and hidden lineage.
  • Short video teasers, carousel ads, podcast sponsorships, organic social posts.
  • Test creative with variations of mystery versus emotional appeal; prioritise high retention metrics.

Launch

2 weeks around launch (conversion)

  • Deepen engagement with readers through historical context and author storytelling.
  • Blog essays, YouTube shorts, newsletter interviews, influencer reviews.
  • Leverage dwell time and engagement signals to remarket to active readers.

Post-Launch

6 weeks post-launch (sustain momentum)

  • Drive purchases via emotional CTAs and limited-time offers tied to heritage themes.
  • Amazon A+ copy, BookBub ads, direct email campaigns, paid social retargeting.
  • Automate high-performing ad sets and A/B test cover imagery and CTA language.
